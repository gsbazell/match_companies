## 1. Brand Strategy

### Rationale

Our brand embodies connection — bridging function and emotion, challenge and solution, seller and buyer. By blending B2B expertise with B2C storytelling, we craft a human and credible B2B brand that inspires, leads with insight, and makes complex solutions feel clear, accessible, and valuable.

### Brand Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Function meets emotion** | Simplifying complexity through intuitive, human-centered solutions. |
| **Embody connection** | Bridging systems, people, and relationships to create seamless B2B experiences. |
| **Touch of B2C** | Combining B2B expertise with B2C storytelling that inspires trust and drives growth. |

### Brand Vision

We envision a world where every B2B experience feels personal and connected. Sana bridges the gap between business systems and human relationships, simplifying complexity, amplifying collaboration, and enabling companies to grow together.

### Brand Mission

We empower B2B organizations to sell smarter, serve faster, and connect more deeply through commerce solutions that integrate seamlessly, simplify the complex, and strengthen lasting relationships.

### Brand Tagline

> **Creating a new future of connected commerce**

### Brand Visualization

Sana Commerce is where technology meets empathy. We engineer B2B solutions that make businesses work smarter and feel more human. Every product, pixel, and post builds one thing: **connection** — enabling our customers to serve their own customers better, bridging the buyer and seller relationship.

Key relationship model:
- **People:** Seller (our client) ↔ Buyer (end client)
- **Expertise:** B2B, ERP, Logistics, PIM, Orders, Webstore
- **Core:** Connection between Seller and Buyer through the Expert layer

## 2. Brand Voice

### Rationale

Our brand voice brings B2C creativity to B2B — bold, playful, and insightful — to make complex topics clear, engaging, and actionable. We speak our customers' language, shaping every message around their needs and mindset to ensure it truly resonates.

### Voice Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Engaging & Playful** | We make complex ideas engaging by framing through the customer's lens and real-world examples. |
| **Disruptive & Bold** | Challenge conventions and stand out in B2B content through B2C-like creativity and emotional impact. |
| **Meaningful & Actionable** | Deliver insights that educate, inspire, and drive results. |

### Approach

Prioritize minimal, high-impact execution over large, costly campaigns. Continuously test and refine content through A/B tests and feedback. The voice stays relevant, adaptive, and effective.

### Tonation

We communicate like the experts we are — approachable but precise, human but structured. Our goal is not to sound friendly for the sake of it, but to connect clearly, consistently, and confidently.

#### Functional Tone

| Trait | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Concise** | Communicate clearly and directly. |
| **Actionable** | Focus on practical outcomes. |
| **Positive** | Focus on possibilities, not problems. |
| **Disruptive** | Challenge conventions with bold ideas. |
| **Progressive** | Stay modern and adaptable, always improving. |

#### Emotional Tone

| Trait | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Human** | Connect with people, celebrate wins. |
| **Engaging** | Attract attention and inspire engagement. |
| **Playful** | Make complex topics pleasantly memorable. |
| **Bold** | Confident, clear, unafraid to stand out in B2B. |
| **Confident** | Lead with expertise and always aim higher. |

### Tone Spectrum

The more **generic** (one-to-all) the communication → more business-conventional and formal.
The more **personal** (one-to-one) the communication → more individual, engaging, and B2C-style.

- **Business-facing:** Disruptive → Positive → Actionable
- **Customer-facing:** Bold → Playful → Engaging

## 3. Content

### Rationale

Our content brings the Sana Commerce brand to life by turning challenges into clear, actionable solutions. It is bold, playful, and engaging, connecting with our audience as trusted business partners and guiding them toward meaningful next steps.

### Content Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Structured for Clarity** | Introduce the need, offer the solution, close with a concrete takeaway. |
| **Bonding with Audience** | Engage the audience by turning real challenges into clear, actionable Q&A solutions. |
| **Relevant and Practical** | Use relatable industry scenarios to reinforce solutions. |

### Content Principles

#### Functional Content

| Principle | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Questioning** | Ask the right questions to uncover real needs. |
| **Answering** | Respond with clarity, empathy, and purpose. |
| **Situational** | Bridge buyer and seller through shared value. |
| **Solutions** | We always bring answers that create progress. |
| **Relatable** | Present the challenge, solution, and next action. |
| **Actionable** | Show our impact — how we create real change. |
| **Industry** | Always grounded in real manufacturing needs. |
| **Storytelling** | Use real case stories to make ideas relatable. |

#### Emotional Content

| Principle | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Expert** | Leverage Sana experts to build insight and trust. |
| **Immersive** | Blend B2C energy with B2B substance. |

### Content Structure (Every Piece Must Follow This)

Every communication follows this structure:

```
1. NEED      → Start with a real challenge or question
2. SOLUTION  → Present the Sana solution
3. TAKEAWAY  → End with a direct call-to-action

Supporting elements:
- Tagline
- Logo
- Media (imagery/visuals)
```

**Example:**

| Element | Seller | Buyer | Expert |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Need (Heading)** | Can tariffs actually work in your favor? | Is your buyer ready to beat trade fees and cut costs? | Need help to compete trade barriers? |
| **Solution (Paragraph)** | Let's simplify B2B buying with PunchOut. | Let's keep rolling with SanaPay. | Let's optimize your order and inventory flow. |
| **Takeaway (CTA)** | Explore PunchOut | Calculate savings | Talk to [expert] |

### Content Taxonomy

When creating content: **Pick a persona → Choose a relatable theme → Connect to a product/solution → Place a CTA.**

## 5. Logo

### Rationale

The logo embodies the dialogue at the heart of our brand — a balance of opposites that belong together. It reflects the harmony between logic and emotion, precision and warmth, system and story. It represents **connection**.

### Logo Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **Logomark** | The shorthand of our brand — simple, recognizable, human. Inspired by apostrophes symbolizing communication. Reflects the connection between technology and people. |
| **Logotype** | Generally not used without the logomark. Use the approved logotype instead of typing the company name (except in live text). |
| **Lockup** | The structured combination of logomark + logotype. Most common form. Use for external applications. |

### Logo Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Clear space** | Minimum space = height of the logomark. No text, graphics, or other logos may enter this area. |
| **Minimum size** | Not below 150px wide / 32px high on screen. |
| **Product alignment** | Align product names with the logotype. Maintain clear space using the logomark. |
| **Partner logos** | Separate with a vertical line using Uber Move Display Light at the same height as the logo. |
| **Tagline placement** | Must always appear with the Sana logo. Height matches the logo's x-height. Can be placed on any side, aligned to a corner. |
| **Placement** | Can be centered, left-, or right-aligned at top, center, or bottom of a page. Use halves/columns for positioning. |

### Logo Color Usage

- **Primary:** Solid red logo on white or light-colored backgrounds.
- **Quiet presence** (website header, letterhead, business card): Use on primary black or white backgrounds.
- **Loud presence** (billboard, advertisement): May pair with primary brand colors.

### Logo Don'ts

- Do NOT use the dark logo on a dark background (reduces contrast).
- Do NOT combine more than one color in the wordmark and logomark.
- Do NOT change the proportions of the logo.
- Do NOT scale the logomark separately from the wordmark.

### Rationale

The gradient adds depth and dimension — creating a dynamic and modern look. It reinforces the connection between the human (warm) and business (cool) aspects of the brand.

### Gradient Colors

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Sana Heart** | `#EB0F37` | Warm accent in gradient |
| **Sana Azure** | `#5664F9` | Primary gradient color |
| **Sana Cobalt** | `#222277` | **Gradient use ONLY** — never standalone |

### Gradient Directions

| Variant | Description |
|---|---|
| **Gradient 01** | Spotlight from top-right — depth and focus |
| **Gradient 02** | Light from bottom-right — grounded, uplifting |
| **Gradient 03** | Light from bottom-left — greater intensity |
| **Gradient 04** | Spotlight from top-left — clarity and forward motion |

### CSS Gradient (Online Use)

Base: linear gradient from Sana Azure (`#5664F9`) to Sana Cobalt (`#222277`), with radial Sana Heart (`#EB0F37`) overlays at varying opacities (0%–60%–100%) and feathering.

### Print Gradient (Illustrator)

1. Background: linear gradient Azure → Cobalt (0°)
2. Layer 2: Large circle, Feather 250pt, linear gradient Heart (0% → 60% opacity, 110°)
3. Layer 3: Oval (75% height), Feather 250pt, linear gradient Heart (0% → 60% opacity, 110°)
4. Layer 4: Circle, Feather 250pt, linear gradient Heart (100% → 0% opacity at 40%, 110°)

## 8. Typography

### Rationale

Typography is a key part of our brand identity. PP Neue Montreal shapes the tone of our words — reflecting who we are and what we stand for.

### Typeface

| Weight | Usage |
|---|---|
| **PP Neue Montreal Medium** | Headings, emphasis, UI labels |
| **PP Neue Montreal Book** | Body text, paragraphs, descriptions |

PP Neue Montreal is a modern, geometric sans-serif that balances precision with a humanist touch. Use italics for emphasis only.

### Type Scale

| Size (px) | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 130% | 0% | Fine print |
| 10 | 130% | 0% | Captions |
| 12 | 130% | 0% | Small body |
| 14 | 130% | 0% | Body text |
| 16 | 130% | 0% | Body text (default) |
| 20 | 130% | 0% | Large body / Intro |
| 24 | 130% | -0.01em | Subheading |
| 32 | 120% | -0.01em | Heading 4 |
| 40 | 120% | -0.01em | Heading 3 |
| 48 | 120% | -0.01em | Heading 2 |
| 56 | 120% | -0.01em | Heading 1 |
| 64 | 120% | -0.01em | Display |
| 72 | 120% | -0.01em | Display large |
| 80–120 | 110% | -0.02em | Hero / Feature |

**Rule of thumb:** Line-height decreases as text size increases. Body text gets more breathing room (130%), headings are tighter (110–120%).

### Kerning

Always use **Metric** kerning.

### Typography Don'ts

- Do NOT use extremely tall or tight leading.
- Do NOT set headlines in ALL CAPS — use **Sentence Case**.
- Do NOT mix weights or fonts within a single headline.
- Do NOT use overly tight or wide tracking.

## 10. Imagery / Photography

### Rationale

Photography puts people first — capturing real moments with warmth and humanity. It shows the people behind our technology, emphasizing connection, approachability, and trust.

### Photography Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **People First** | Prioritize people in every image, showing them as important and central. |
| **Depth of Field** | Use focus and blur to create visual hierarchy and emphasize key subjects. |
| **Rhythm of Three** | Arrange elements in groups of three for balance and natural visual flow. |

### People First Rules

- Always feature **minimum two people** if feasible.
- People should **never look directly at the camera** (authenticity).
- Use the **Hero / Hub / Highlight / Hygiene** framework:
  - **Hero:** Wide shot, clear context, minimal distractions, people visible in action.
  - **Hub:** Portraits or scenarios showcasing persona/industry in action.
  - **Highlight:** Detail shots with shallow depth of field.
  - **Hygiene:** Real people, real emotions, relatable moments.

### Photography Do's

- Use natural light or natural-looking light.
- Highlight the subject clearly.
- Use light to create warmth, depth, and atmosphere.
- Use shadows thoughtfully for dimension.
- Use composition with the 3x Grid.
- Show real people in authentic manufacturing/B2B environments.
- Subtly adjust small elements to align with Sana brand colors.

### Photography Don'ts

- **No AI-generated people** — use only real people.
- No images lacking depth of field (too busy).
- No unnatural environments.
- No staged or cliche shots.
- No harsh, artificial, or inconsistent lighting.
- No overexposure washing out details.
- No dominant off-brand colors.
- No over-saturation.
- No color overlays or artificial Photoshop enhancements.
- No cluttered images with no clear subject.

### Perspectives

| Perspective | Usage |
|---|---|
| **Aerial / Bird's Eye** | Hero content — show scale, impact, context from above |
| **Eye-Level** | Hub content — authentic, equal, face-to-face |
| **Close-Up** | Hygiene content — human action, detail, interaction |
| **Dynamic** | Movement and energy — engaging and relatable |

### Industries for Photography

Manufacturing, Machinery, Distribution, Chemical, Automotive, Construction, Electronics, Medical, Wholesale.

## 11. Data Visualization

### Rationale

Data visualizations should be **minimal and clear**, accurately conveying data without misleading or distorting.

### Data Viz Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Balance layout** | Use the central X and Y axis |
| **Stacking** | Create a sense of connection through stacking |
| **Rhythm of three** | Use rhythm of three for defining design direction |

### Chart Color Palette

| Level | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (1st) | `#12123F` | Main data series |
| Secondary (2nd) | `#5664F9` | Second data series |
| Tertiary (3rd) | `#AAB2FC` | Third data series |
| Quaternary (4th) | `#BFC5FD` | Fourth data series |
| Quinary (5th) | `#D5D8FE` | Fifth data series |

> **Key rule:** Use **blue as the main color** and **red as accent only**. Blue symbolizes knowledge; the focus is on conveying information.

### Chart Styling

| Element | Style |
|---|---|
| Title | `#12123F` (Sana Indigo) |
| Value Labels | `#5664F9` (Sana Azure) |
| Caption | `#A5A49F` (gray) |
| Category Labels | `#A5A49F` (gray) |
| Gridlines (X & Y) | `#D6D5D1`, 1px |
| Border / Stroke | `#D6D5D1`, 1px |

### Chart Layout Rules

- Place charts within a **stroked container** to anchor them in the layout.
- **24px** between stroke and chart content.
- **32px** between chart edges and any data visualization.
- **50px** padding from container edges.
- Bar width should be **twice the spacing** between bars.

### Chart Types

- **Bar and Column:** Categorical data, ordinal comparisons.
- **Stacked Bar and Column:** Group relationships and subcategory breakdowns.
- **Pie and Donut:** Numerical proportions. Donut allows extra data in center.

## 12. Design Concept

### Rationale

Our Modular Design System drives quality and efficiency with fewer resources. It aligns with key personas and marketing goals while uniting product and growth teams. Powered by automation, it builds smarter, faster campaigns.

### Design Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Balance layout** | Use the central X and Y axis |
| **Stacking** | A stacking design system creating a sense of connection |
| **Rhythm of three** | Use rhythm of three hierarchy for defining design direction |

### Layout Types

| Layout | Description |
|---|---|
| **Full Image** | Immersive storytelling through full-frame imagery. Uses stacking and balanced overlays. Ideal for automated, high-impact campaigns. |
| **Vertical Split** | Balanced design along the X-axis. Modular, scalable layout aligning imagery and message. |
| **Horizontal Split** | Connection through the Y-axis and rhythm of three. Combines people, copy, and iconography for cohesive, stackable stories. |
